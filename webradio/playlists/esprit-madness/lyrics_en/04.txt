[Verse 1]
Mr. Whoever lost his hat
He blamed the wind, the dog, the cat
He searched the hall, he checked the stair
It had been sitting on his chair

[Chorus]
Oh, Mr. Whoever, what a day
You chase your shadow down the way
The piano jumps, the brass replies
You find your hat and wave goodbye

[Verse 2]
He left his keys inside the door
Then searched the garden, floor to floor
The whole street joined the grand parade
And brought him lemonade
