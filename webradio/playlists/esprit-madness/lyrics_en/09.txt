[Verse 1]
The market stalls are closing down
Rain is silver on the town
The apples shine beneath the sheet
As puddles gather at our feet

[Chorus]
Rain on the market, rain on the street
A little grey sky and a warm backbeat
We share one umbrella as the day moves on
And hum our way till the clouds are gone

[Verse 2]
The flower seller packs away
A final rose to end the day
The saxophone sings soft and slow
Through every shining street below
