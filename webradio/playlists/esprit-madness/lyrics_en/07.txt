[Verse 1]
The neighbours gather by the wall
Someone brings a battered ball
The little band begins to play
And pulls the whole street out to stay

[Chorus]
Hey, hey, the whole block sings
Clap your hands and shake the strings
Side by side beneath the sky
Raise your voices, let them fly

[Verse 2]
The windows open one by one
The evening turns to heaps of fun
No one asks who knows the tune
We sing it back beneath the moon
