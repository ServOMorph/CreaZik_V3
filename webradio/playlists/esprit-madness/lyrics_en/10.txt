[Verse 1]
The banners fall, the lights grow dim
The last brass note becomes a hymn
We stack the chairs and sweep the floor
Then hear one song and ask for more

[Chorus]
One more round before we go
One last step beneath the glow
When the morning finds us here
We'll remember every cheer

[Verse 2]
The dawn is waiting down the lane
Our coats are damp with silver rain
We wave goodbye, but make a plan
To meet again with the band
