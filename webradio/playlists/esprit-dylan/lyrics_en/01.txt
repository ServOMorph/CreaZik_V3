[Verse 1]
I took the road at break of day
With my old guitar and little to say
Fence posts blurred like passing years
The north wind taught my song to carry

[Chorus]
The road runs long, the road runs wide
We walk toward morning, side by side
Don't ask me where I'll rest tonight
I was born to chase the light

[Verse 2]
An old man spoke beside the station bar
Of all he'd lost and all he'd won so far
He laughed at storms and let tears fall thin
Then said, young man, keep walking in
