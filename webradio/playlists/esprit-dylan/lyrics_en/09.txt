[Verse 1]
The night train races toward the south
I left my loneliness behind
The wheels keep singing on the steel
A tune I thought I'd lost for good

[Chorus]
Roll on, roll on, night train
Carry me past the old refrain
Roll on, roll on, toward the dawn
I'll step off when the road is gone

[Verse 2]
The conductor asks me for my fare
I hand him words I wrote somewhere
He smiles and tucks them in his coat
Then lets me ride on hope
