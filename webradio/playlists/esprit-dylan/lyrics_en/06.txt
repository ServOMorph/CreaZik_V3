[Verse 1]
Rain falls tonight along the line
The border guards play cards till nine
In my pocket, one faded face
Still waits on the other side of place

[Chorus]
Rain on the border, rain in my heart
Each drop counts the hours apart
I'll cross the river when daylight comes
Rain, wait for me where the road runs

[Verse 2]
The river's wide and the boat is old
The ferryman sleeps; the moon keeps watch
I have my voice, I have this song
To pay the price and carry on
